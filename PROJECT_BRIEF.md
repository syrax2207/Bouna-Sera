# 1. Project Brief

## 1.1 Project overview

Create a bespoke website concept for **Buona Sera Cafe**, identified by the user as a prospect in Sector 104, Noida.

The website should help a visitor understand the venue, explore its food, view its atmosphere and take an action such as getting directions or contacting the business.

The prototype is intended for private review and potential sale to the business owner. It must not imply that VEYRO has been appointed by the cafe or that the website is official.

## 1.2 Business context and research status

The prospect was discovered through a Google Maps/local search for cafes in Sector 104, Noida. Previous desk-research discussion referenced third-party business/listing sources, but this documentation does not treat unverified details from those sources as current facts.

The user has identified:
- Business name: Buona Sera Cafe.
- General locality: Sector 104, Noida.
- Opportunity hypothesis: the business may have a weak owned digital presence and may benefit from a dedicated website.

**Important:** The absence of a website has not been conclusively verified in this project pack. Search the business name, address, phone number, social profiles and map listing before approaching the owner. Do not confuse delivery-platform pages, directories or social pages with an official website.

## 1.3 Project objectives

### Primary objectives
1. Create a memorable first impression.
2. Establish a clear and credible digital identity.
3. Present food and menu information elegantly.
4. Show the dining atmosphere through photography.
5. Make location and contact actions effortless.
6. Demonstrate professional design and engineering quality to a potential client.

### Secondary objectives
- Build a portfolio-quality VEYRO case study, clearly labelled as a concept.
- Demonstrate responsive design, interaction design and frontend architecture.
- Create a maintainable structure that could later be adapted into an owner-approved production website.

## 1.4 Target audiences

### Local prospective customers
People searching for a nearby cafe, food options, ambience, directions or contact details.

### Existing customers
People who already know the venue and want quick access to its menu, location, hours or contact information.

### Business owner / decision-maker
A person evaluating whether a dedicated website could improve the business's presentation and customer journey.

## 1.5 Positioning hypothesis

Proposed creative positioning:

**“An Italian-inspired place for good food and unhurried moments.”**

This is a creative hypothesis only. It must be checked against the actual business concept and owner preferences. Do not assert that the cafe is authentically Italian, specializes in Italian cuisine or offers a particular experience unless confirmed.

## 1.6 Core visitor journey

1. Visitor lands on the homepage.
2. Hero communicates the brand and offers a clear next action.
3. Visitor explores featured food or the menu.
4. Visitor views real photos and genuine customer feedback.
5. Visitor checks location and verified operating information.
6. Visitor calls, messages, opens directions or submits an enquiry if supported.

## 1.7 Scope

### Included in first prototype
- Single-page responsive website.
- Navigation and mobile menu.
- Editorial hero.
- Brand introduction.
- Featured food/experience section.
- Interactive menu driven by local data.
- Restaurant/story and atmosphere section.
- Responsive gallery with lightbox.
- Genuine reviews only, if verified.
- Visit/contact section.
- Footer.
- Basic technical SEO.
- Accessibility and performance checks.

### Excluded unless specifically approved
- Online ordering/payment.
- Live reservation engine.
- Customer accounts.
- Admin dashboard/CMS.
- Backend database.
- Loyalty programme.
- Delivery integrations.
- Automated review collection.
- Claims of measurable sales or SEO outcomes.

## 1.8 Definition of done

The prototype is ready for owner review when:
- All ten sections are implemented coherently.
- All visible controls work or are clearly non-interactive.
- Mobile, tablet and desktop layouts are checked.
- No unverified business facts are presented as true.
- Placeholder images/content are clearly tracked.
- Menu filters and detail views work.
- Gallery lightbox works with keyboard and touch.
- Contact and directions links use verified destinations only.
- Production build succeeds.
- Accessibility and performance issues are reviewed.
