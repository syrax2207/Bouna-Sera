# BATCH 8 — QUALITY ASSURANCE, SEO & LAUNCH READINESS

Audit the complete project.

Test:
- Desktop, tablet and mobile.
- Navigation and mobile drawer.
- All CTAs.
- Menu filters/search/modal.
- Gallery/lightbox/keyboard/touch.
- Contact and map destinations.
- Focus states and keyboard access.
- Reduced-motion behavior.
- Contrast and semantic heading structure.
- Image loading, dimensions and layout shifts.
- Console errors and broken links.
- Production build.

SEO:
- Unique title and meta description.
- Open Graph metadata.
- Canonical only when final domain is known.
- Semantic headings.
- Descriptive alt text.
- LocalBusiness structured data using verified fields only.
- Sitemap and robots.txt where appropriate.

Do not claim rankings, traffic or conversion improvements. Fix critical issues and document anything that remains. Do not redesign working sections without a specific defect.
