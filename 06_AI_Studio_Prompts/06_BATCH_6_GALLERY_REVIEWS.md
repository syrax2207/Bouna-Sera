# BATCH 6 — GALLERY & CUSTOMER REVIEWS

Implement an asymmetric responsive gallery and accessible lightbox.

Gallery requirements:
- Responsive grid/composition.
- Next/previous.
- Escape to close.
- Arrow-key navigation.
- Touch support where practical.
- Image counter.
- Alt text.
- Lazy loading.
- Correct image dimensions to reduce layout shift.

Use genuine business photographs or clearly tracked licensed placeholders. Do not imply stock images show the real cafe.

Add customer reviews only if genuine review content, rating and source attribution are verified. Otherwise omit the review section or replace it with a non-testimonial visual section.
