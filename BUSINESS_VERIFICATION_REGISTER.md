## 7.3 Source hierarchy

1. Direct owner confirmation.
2. Current official business website/social account.
3. Current Google Business Profile controlled by business.
4. Current menu supplied by business.
5. Reputable third-party listings as leads only.

Third-party directory pages may be outdated, incomplete or user-generated. Never treat them as authoritative for sensitive business facts.

## 7.4 Website gap verification

Search:
- Exact business name.
- Name + Sector 104 Noida.
- Phone number in quotes.
- Exact address.
- Instagram bio links.
- Facebook profile links.
- Google Business Profile website field.
- Search results for likely domains.

Distinguish:
- Official standalone website.
- Delivery platform.
- Food directory.
- Social media profile.
- Map listing.

If a functioning official site exists, reassess the opportunity rather than claiming no website.

## 7.5 Image register

For every image, record:
- File name.
- What it depicts.
- Source/owner.
- Permission/licence.
- Whether it is real venue photography or placeholder.
- Alt text.
- Intended crop.

Never present generic stock imagery as an actual Buona Sera interior or dish.
