## 2. Why this concept exists

The project explores how a café website can provide a stronger owned digital experience than relying only on third-party listings and social profiles. The proposed website should make it easier for a visitor to understand the café, explore its food and atmosphere, find practical visit information, and contact the business using verified channels.

The website is also intended to demonstrate VEYRO's capabilities in:
- Art-directed responsive web design.
- Editorial storytelling and food-led visual presentation.
- Interactive menu and gallery experiences.
- Thoughtful motion and micro-interactions.
- Mobile-first conversion paths.
- Accessibility, performance and local SEO fundamentals.

## 4. Audiences to design for

These are working audience hypotheses, not confirmed customer demographics:

1. **Nearby residents and workers** looking for a convenient place to eat or meet.
2. **Couples and friends** exploring cafés for a relaxed outing.
3. **Families and small groups** who want to inspect the menu and practical visit details.
4. **Brunch and coffee visitors** looking for food and drinks.
5. **First-time visitors** who need location, timings and contact information.

Design for quick decision-making on mobile. Avoid assuming age, income, lifestyle or other demographic details without evidence.

## 5. Core experience proposition

A warm, contemporary café experience presented through:
- Food-led, high-quality imagery.
- A concise introduction to the café.
- Easy-to-scan menu categories.
- A clear gallery or atmosphere section.
- Accurate location and visit details.
- A simple contact or enquiry action.

The working creative phrase **“Good food. Slow moments. Buona Sera.”** is a proposed concept line only. It is not an official slogan.
