# SEO & Local Discovery Plan

## 1. Principle

Use the system `applet_seo` skill. SEO must describe the actual business accurately. Do not use structured data to publish unverified information.

## 2. Pre-launch requirements

- Confirm the official business name.
- Confirm address and map pin.
- Confirm phone and opening hours.
- Confirm actual website/domain ownership and approval.
- Confirm menu and service details.
- Confirm official social profile URLs.

## 3. On-page SEO

- One clear page title.
- One descriptive meta description.
- One primary H1 describing the café and location naturally.
- Logical H2 section headings.
- Descriptive image alt text.
- Human-readable URL if a multi-page structure is later introduced.
- Internal anchor links for one-page navigation.
- Fast, mobile-friendly page experience.

Do not keyword-stuff location or cuisine phrases.

## 4. Local SEO

Potential local phrases can be considered only after confirming the business's location and actual offering, for example:
- Cafe in Sector 104, Noida.
- Restaurant/cafe near Hajipur, Noida.

Use only accurate categories and cuisine descriptions. Do not claim “best”, “top-rated” or other superiority without substantiation.

## 5. Structured data

Use appropriate `Restaurant` or `CafeOrCoffeeShop` structured data only when the business identity and fields are verified. Include only supported properties. Do not invent:
- Aggregate ratings or review counts.
- Price range.
- Opening hours.
- Menu URL.
- Telephone.
- Geo coordinates.
- Reservation URL.

Validate JSON-LD before release.

## 6. Social metadata

Create Open Graph and social preview metadata after a final approved visual is available. Do not use an unofficial logo or misleading photograph.

## 7. Indexing and launch

A private concept should not be indexed as an official business website. Before public release:
- Obtain owner approval.
- Confirm domain and deployment ownership.
- Review privacy/legal requirements.
- Ensure all factual content is verified.
- Set canonical URL correctly.
- Configure sitemap/robots only for the approved public site.

## 8. Measurement

Do not add analytics or tracking to the concept without a clear need and appropriate consent/privacy handling. Do not report leads, bookings or revenue unless measured with permission and real data.
