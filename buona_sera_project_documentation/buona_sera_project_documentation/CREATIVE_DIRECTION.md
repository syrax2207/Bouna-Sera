# Creative Direction

## 1. Creative intent

Create a warm, contemporary and editorial café experience. The design should feel inviting and sensory without becoming rustic cliché, generic luxury, or an over-animated template.

**Working mood:** warm evenings, considered hospitality, good food, natural materials, intimate conversation and relaxed confidence.

**Proposed concept line:** “Good food. Slow moments. Buona Sera.”  
This is a design proposal, not an official tagline.

## 2. Visual personality

- Warm, welcoming and composed.
- Editorial rather than template-like.
- Food-led and photography-driven.
- Contemporary with subtle Mediterranean/Italian-inspired cues, without pretending to represent the business's official heritage.
- Premium in craft, not ostentatious.
- Strong hierarchy and generous breathing room.

## 3. Suggested palette direction

These are provisional design tokens, not existing café brand colours:

| Role | Suggested value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Warm ivory | `#F4F0E8` | Main light background |
| Deep olive | `#303B2F` | Primary dark surfaces / accents |
| Terracotta | `#B76545` | Small emphasis accents |
| Espresso | `#241F1B` | Main text / deep contrast |
| Muted sage | `#A5AA91` | Secondary surfaces |
| Soft white | `#FCFAF6` | Cards / content surfaces |

Check contrast before finalising actual pairings. Do not apply all colours at equal intensity.

## 4. Typography direction

Use a restrained two-family system:
- Expressive editorial serif for selected display headings.
- Highly legible sans-serif for body, controls and navigation.

Use a limited type scale and consistent line lengths. Avoid excessive uppercase, tiny labels and long blocks of centred text.

Candidate font pairings are subject to availability, licensing and actual visual review. Do not load multiple font families unnecessarily.

## 5. Composition

- Large editorial hero with strong food/space image.
- Asymmetric image and text layouts where they improve storytelling.
- Clear section rhythm with alternating light/dark surfaces.
- Strong menu typography and disciplined category controls.
- Avoid excessive card grids, pill-shaped controls and decorative badges.
- Keep the main call-to-action visually clear.

## 6. Motion character

Motion should feel smooth, restrained and intentional:
- Short entrance reveals for key content.
- Subtle image movement or scale on hover where appropriate.
- Smooth menu category transitions.
- Thoughtful lightbox transitions.
- Clear button and navigation feedback.

Avoid scroll hijacking, constant floating objects, long intro sequences, parallax that harms mobile performance, and animation that delays access to content.

## 7. Imagery

Priority order:
1. Owner-approved real café photography.
2. Licensed stock photography clearly used as illustrative concept material.
3. AI-generated concept imagery clearly disclosed in internal/demo context and never presented as actual café photography.

Images should have consistent art direction, natural-looking food, believable lighting and useful crops. Avoid fake logos, fake staff, fake customers, fabricated interiors or misleading depictions of the actual premises.

## 8. Design quality bar

The site should look art-directed at first glance, but every visual decision must support content, usability or brand atmosphere. Avoid generic AI-generated design patterns and ornamental interaction without purpose.
